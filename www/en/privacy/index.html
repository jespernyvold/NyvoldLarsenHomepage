Privacy and analytics

Last updated 1 August 2026

Nyvold Larsen ApS is responsible for the processing described on this page. Questions can be sent to jesper@nyvold-larsen.dk.

Information this site processes

The site can be used without an account and without providing personal information. If you contact Nyvold Larsen by email, the information in your message is used to respond to your enquiry and manage any subsequent business relationship.

Analytics

Google Analytics is only loaded after you actively accept analytics. If you decline, no analytics request is sent. When enabled, analytics is used to understand visits, article views, downloads and contact-link activity. Reports describe consenting visitors and are not a complete count of all site use.

The analytics configuration disables advertising signals and personalization. A Google Analytics measurement ID must be configured before analytics can operate.

Your choice

Your analytics preference and selected language can be stored in your browser’s local storage. These functional preferences stay on your device. Use “Analytics settings” in the footer to change or withdraw your analytics choice at any time.

Service providers and retention

When analytics is accepted, Google processes analytics information on behalf of Nyvold Larsen. The final production configuration, retention period and legal wording will be reviewed before launch.
