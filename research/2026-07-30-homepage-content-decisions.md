# Homepage content decisions

Date: 2026-07-30

Status: Agreed content direction; no implementation

## Agreed homepage structure

1. Opening
2. Engage me when
3. What I do
4. My background
5. Insights
6. Start a conversation

## 1. Opening

### Making technology strategy matter

I help organizations with complex, business-critical technology landscapes establish a clear direction—and turn it into decisions, action and lasting change.

I work with executive teams as an adviser or interim leader, connecting strategy, architecture and execution.

## 2. Engage me when

- A transformation has lost momentum or is not delivering the expected value
- Technology and architecture complexity is obstructing business priorities
- A consequential decision must be made about platforms, vendors or strategic direction
- Cybersecurity, resilience or regulatory obligations require a practical response
- Experienced technology or architecture leadership is needed for a critical period

## 3. What I do

### IT Strategy & Architecture

Turn business priorities into clear technology direction, target architectures and actionable roadmaps.

### Transformation & Modernization

Lead complex transformation initiatives—from legacy modernization and platform evolution to simplification and technical debt reduction.

### Software Product Development

Strengthen software product development by aligning business needs, product direction, architecture and engineering execution.

### Interim & Fractional Leadership

Provide experienced, hands-on leadership for software, technology and architecture teams during periods of change.

## 4. My background

Keep the existing "My background" text unchanged for now.

## 5. Insights

The Insights section will provide a concise overview of all published articles. The initial articles are:

- Technical Debt
- Digital Sovereignty

The articles will support the subjects in "What I do" and demonstrate experience and perspective rather than function as a general-purpose blog.

A subject may be supported by several articles, and an article may support more than one subject.

## 6. Start a conversation

Close with a short invitation for organizations that recognize their situation in the homepage content. Exact wording has not yet been agreed.

## Supporting decisions

- The homepage should remain brief and precise.
- Articles should provide depth and evidence behind the concise service descriptions.
- Cybersecurity remains relevant across strategy, transformation and the "Engage me when" section, but it is not one of the four primary "What I do" subjects.
- Software Product Development replaces Cybersecurity & Resilience as a primary subject because it more strongly reflects the depth of the professional background.
- The positioning is aimed at organizations with complex, business-critical technology landscapes rather than at a particular industry.
