# Nyvold Larsen homepage — look and feel research

Date: 30 July 2026

Status: Approved design direction

Company: Nyvold Larsen ApS

## Purpose

This document records the agreed visual and structural direction for the new
Nyvold Larsen homepage. It is a design brief and research record, not a
technical implementation specification.

The existing logo and core website content must be reused. The presentation
will be redesigned to feel considerably more professional, credible and
purposeful.

## Existing positioning

Nyvold Larsen is positioned as a senior, principal-led technology advisory
practice working with financial institutions and other complex organisations.

The principal service areas are:

1. IT Strategy & Architecture
2. Transformation & Modernisation
3. Cybersecurity & Resilience
4. Interim / Fractional Leadership

The central value proposition is helping organisations navigate complex
technology decisions and critical change.

Important credibility signals from the existing site:

- More than 20 years of experience in large and complex IT organisations
- Senior leadership experience in the financial sector
- Former CTO at SDC
- Responsibility for technical strategy and architecture for a SaaS banking
  platform serving more than 50 Nordic banks
- Direct, executive-level advice and hands-on leadership

## References considered

Five websites were considered as possible visual references:

1. [Cavalier Consulting](https://cavalier-consulting.com/) — premium,
   authoritative boutique technology advisory
2. [Tinly Consulting](https://tinly.se/) — restrained Scandinavian consulting
   design
3. [Manyone](https://manyone.com/) — bold, contemporary strategy and
   technology presentation
4. [Willowbark Consulting](https://willowbark.com/) — clear, principal-led CTO
   advisory structure
5. [Implement Consulting Group](https://implementconsultinggroup.com/) — warm,
   editorial Nordic consulting style

## Approved foundation

The approved foundation is **Willowbark Consulting**.

Willowbark was selected because its structure closely matches the Nyvold
Larsen business model:

- It explains complex technical services in plain business language.
- The visitor can quickly understand who the advisor helps.
- Engagement scenarios are presented before detailed service descriptions.
- Services are clearly separated and easy to scan.
- Personal experience and principal-led delivery create trust.
- Calls to action are direct without feeling aggressively commercial.

The new site should be inspired by these principles, but must not copy
Willowbark's branding, wording or individual layouts.

## Agreed look and feel

The homepage should feel:

- Senior and credible
- Clear and direct
- Calm rather than flashy
- Personal, but not informal
- Modern without looking like a software start-up
- Appropriate for financial institutions, executives and regulated
  organisations

The site should avoid:

- Generic stock imagery of code, servers or anonymous office meetings
- Bright technology gradients and excessive animation
- Dense walls of text
- Visual language associated with a large implementation agency
- Claims that make the one-person, principal-led practice look artificially
  large

## Visual direction

### Colour

Use a restrained, mostly neutral palette derived from the existing monochrome
logo:

- Warm white or very light neutral page background
- Charcoal or near-black primary text
- Soft grey secondary surfaces
- One restrained accent colour if required
- Dark sections may be used to separate the four advisory services

Exact colour values remain to be chosen during implementation.

### Typography

Use an editorial serif typeface for important headlines and a clean sans-serif
typeface for body copy, navigation and labels.

The typography should create hierarchy through scale, spacing and contrast
rather than heavy font weights or decoration.

### Photography

Use the existing professional portrait of Jesper Nyvold Larsen as a central
trust element. It should be recropped and colour-treated to fit the new design.

Photography should support the principal-led nature of the practice. Additional
imagery is optional and should only be introduced when it adds meaning.

### Layout

- Generous whitespace
- Short, focused text blocks
- Strong opening statement
- Clear section labels
- Numbered engagement scenarios and service areas
- Light dividers rather than excessive cards and boxes
- Responsive single-column presentation on mobile
- A consistent primary call to action

## Proposed homepage structure

### 1. Header

- Existing Nyvold Larsen Consulting logo
- Minimal navigation
- Primary action: “Start a conversation”

### 2. Hero

Suggested headline:

> Clarity for complex technology decisions and critical change.

Supporting text should explain that Nyvold Larsen advises financial
institutions and complex organisations on architecture, strategy,
transformation, modernisation and cybersecurity.

The hero includes:

- Professional portrait
- Primary contact action
- Secondary link to advisory services

### 3. Credibility strip

Three concise proof points:

- 20+ years in complex IT organisations
- Former CTO at SDC
- Executive-level advice and hands-on leadership

### 4. Engage me when

Present the situations that make the service relevant:

1. A transformation is stalled or has not delivered the expected results.
2. The architecture landscape has become complex and difficult to navigate.
3. A major technology, platform, vendor or strategy decision must be made.
4. Interim or fractional technology leadership is needed during a critical
   period.

### 5. Advisory services

Present the four services as distinct, scannable areas:

1. IT Strategy & Architecture
2. Transformation & Modernisation
3. Cybersecurity & Resilience
4. Interim / Fractional Leadership

This section may use a dark background to create a strong visual transition.

### 6. Background and personal credibility

Introduce Jesper Nyvold Larsen and explain:

- Relevant leadership background
- Experience from complex organisations
- Former CTO responsibility at SDC
- The benefit of working directly with the senior advisor throughout the
  engagement

Include a link to LinkedIn.

### 7. Contact

End with one calm, direct invitation:

> Let’s clarify the decision, the risks and the way forward.

Primary contact method: `jesper@nyvold-larsen.dk`

## Mock-up decision

A non-production layout mock-up was prepared and approved as the conceptual
direction. It demonstrated:

- A restrained header using the existing logo
- Editorial headline typography
- A split hero with portrait
- A three-part credibility strip
- Numbered engagement scenarios
- A dark four-service section
- A principal-led background section
- A simple final contact invitation

The mock-up is a layout reference only. Typography, responsive behaviour,
accessibility, final copy, imagery and visual details must be refined during
design and implementation.
